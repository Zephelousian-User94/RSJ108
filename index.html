Joss Imhauser's Guide for Good Oral Hygiene

An Instruction Challenge for JOUR108

How to practice good oral hygiene:

Time Commitment (per session): 15 minutes
Cleanliness Rating: 100%

Oral hygiene is a simple yet very important aspect in one's personal cleanliness and health. Failure to practice good oral hygiene can mean thousands of dollars in dental work if one were to mismanage their oral health, like to the point of needing 4 root canals before age 21... This is a step-by-step guide to avoid such a scenario.

  • Cleaning stuff required:

  • Tooth Brush
  • Tooth Paste
  • Cup (or Hands)
  • Water
  • Flossing Tool
  • Mouthwash (Hydrogen Peroxide, oral rince, etc.)

  1. Pre-Wash with Tooth Brush:

    Clean up the bigger dirty particles in your mouth to make way for a more effective clean in step 2.

    - Ensure to cover your teeth, tongue, and gums.

    - Do not use tooth paste for this first step.

  2. Wash-Proper with Tooth Paste:

    Taught during childhood (hopefully), this is where you do the actual brushing and cleaning of your teeth.

    - Brush your teeth, tongue, and gums, with tooth paste.

    - Do this for ~3-7 minutes.

    - Spit out as much foam or tooth paste content from your mouth.

    - DO NOT rinse with water, yet.

  3. Floss with a Flossing Tool:

    Before rinsing, use a plier or the classic string to put the thin line in the gaps of your teeth and move back and forth to remove any gunk between them.

    - If bleeding occurs, do not worry unless it's very severe.

    - Floss every gap between teeth.

  4. Rinse with Water:

    After waiting several minutes for the flouride to settle on your teeth, remove any remaining foam or tooth paste content from your mouth.

    - Use a cup or have your hands in the shape of a bowl to input water into your mouth.

    - Swoosh it around for ~45 seconds, then spit it out.

  5. Rinse with Mouthwash:

    Whether with hydrogen peroxide or oral rinse, input and swoosh it around your mouth as if you were rinsing with water.

    - Keep mouthwash liquid swooshing for ~45 seconds, then spit it out.

    Example mouthwash product - hydrogen peroxide

  6. Recommended After-Cleaning Procedures:

    Optional things to do afterward, these are quality of life things that I personally do after brushing my teeth.

    - Drink Water: A type of "secondary rinse" for an extra cleanliness feeling.

    - Chapstick: Dry lips are cringe and disgusting; keep them moisturized.

  7. Put on a Smile :D

    With shiny, good looking teeth, put on a smile for the people to make them, and you, feel good about life!
